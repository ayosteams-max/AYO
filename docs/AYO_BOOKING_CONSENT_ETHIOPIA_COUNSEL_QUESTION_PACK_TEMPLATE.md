Every item defaults to `BLOCKED` until a proposal is shown not to conflict:

- ownership/equity/voting/control transfer;
- policy or IP ownership transfer;
- uncontrolled broad/perpetual IP rights;
- signing-key, repository or control-plane surrender;
- third-party control of rider evidence or registry;
- blocked export, migration or independent audit;
- unilateral third-party policy change;
- approval-gate bypass;
- withdrawal authority activating replacement content;
- weakened production prohibition;
- production before prerequisites.

No approval can override a RED conflict.
